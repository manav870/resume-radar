How It Works

Three steps from assignments to actionable feedback.

01

Upload

Place student assignments (TXT, DOCX) into a Google Drive folder. Name files like 27_Mohan.txt for auto-detection.

02

Analyze

Gemini AI evaluates each submission against a 4-criterion rubric: Clarity, Accuracy, Completeness, and Depth.

03

Review

Formatted feedback reports are appended to your Google Doc with scores, error analysis, and next steps.
