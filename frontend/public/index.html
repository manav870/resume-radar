The Deterministic Weighted Scoring Formula

Sample Score: 87.5% Match

Unlike black-box AI tools that output arbitrary percentages, Resume Radar evaluates candidates with an explicit, auditable mathematical formula:

Match Score = (0.40 × ReqSkills) + (0.15 × PrefSkills) + (0.25 × Experience) + (0.10 × Education) + (0.10 × Certifications)
Required Skills (40%)
Mandatory skills verified with semantic alias matching.
Preferred Skills (15%)
Bonus points for nice-to-have capabilities.
Experience (25%)
Years alignment ratio against role minimum.
Education (10%)
Degree level hierarchy (Bachelors, Masters, PhD).
Certifications (10%)
Verified credentials & portfolio evidence.
