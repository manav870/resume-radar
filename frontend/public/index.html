How It Works

Three steps from resumes to actionable hiring decisions.

01

Upload

Place candidate resumes (PDF, DOCX, TXT) into a Google Drive folder, or drag-and-drop directly.

02

Analyze

Gemini AI extracts skills and scores candidates against job requirements using our explainable mathematical formula.

03

Review ranked talent, explore pool analytics, shortlist top candidates, and export Google Docs reports.
